## What happens
Blood pressure changes as the heart and blood vessels adapt to new gravity levels, and stress, poor sleep, pain and dehydration all affect it. One high reading is common; what matters is a pattern over several readings taken at rest.

## What astronauts can do
Measure at rest, seated, after a few quiet minutes, and repeat once if the first reading is high. Note any headache, chest pain or vision change.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
Readings of 180/120 mmHg or more twice in a row, or a high reading with chest pain, severe headache, confusion, weakness or vision change, are an emergency: follow the blood-pressure protocol.
