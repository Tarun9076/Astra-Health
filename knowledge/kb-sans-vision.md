## What happens
Spaceflight Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome (SANS) means changes to the structure of the eye and brain during long missions, probably linked to fluids shifting toward the head. NASA calls it a leading spaceflight health risk. NASA research is testing countermeasures such as thigh cuffs that keep fluid in the legs and a daily B-vitamin supplement, and measuring fluid pressure in the head.

## What astronauts can do
Take part in the scheduled eye checks and report any change in your vision, such as blurring, difficulty focusing up close or a shadow in your sight.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
Any new vision change should be reported the same day. Sudden loss of vision, or vision changes with a severe headache, weakness or confusion, is an emergency.
