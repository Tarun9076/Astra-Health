## What happens
In reduced gravity the sense of thirst is weaker, and the habitat's dry air and exercise increase fluid loss. Even mild dehydration can raise resting heart rate, cause headache and tiredness, and make dizziness on standing worse.

## What astronauts can do
Drink regularly through the day rather than waiting for thirst, aim for the mission's daily fluid target, and drink more on exercise days. Pale urine is a simple check.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If you cannot keep fluids down, or dehydration comes with fainting, confusion or a very fast heartbeat.
