## What happens
When sleep is short, workload is high and stress builds up, the body does not fully recover between days. Typical signs come together: sleep below your normal, resting heart rate above your normal, and feeling tired, flat or irritable. Each one alone can be noise; together they are a pattern worth acting on.

## What astronauts can do
Prioritise sleep for a few nights, keep hydration up, swap hard training for lighter sessions until heart rate settles, and tell the commander if the workload is too high. Recheck resting heart rate on waking.

## How ASTRA HEALTH tracks it
The fatigue-pattern rule needs short sleep, raised resting heart rate and high fatigue or low mood together. The AI insights also look for a steady rise in recovery strain, which can appear a day or two before the rule alert.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If the pattern lasts more than three days, or comes with chest pain, fainting, fever or thoughts of self-harm.
