## What happens
NASA lists injury and reduced performance during spacewalks (EVAs) as a risk, including fatigue, suit-related injury, low oxygen and high carbon dioxide in the suit. Moving from cabin pressure to lower suit pressure can also cause decompression sickness, when nitrogen forms bubbles in body tissues.

## What astronauts can do
Follow the pre-breathe and suit procedures exactly, stay hydrated, and report joint pain, tingling, itching skin or unusual tiredness during or after an EVA.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
Immediately for joint pain, numbness, weakness, breathing difficulty or confusion during or after an EVA.
