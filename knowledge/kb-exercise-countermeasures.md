## What happens
Exercise is the main tool against bone loss, muscle loss and reduced fitness in space. Missions combine resistance training (for muscles and bones) with aerobic exercise (for the heart and lungs), and NASA keeps testing more compact approaches for spacecraft with little room.

## What astronauts can do
Follow your mission profile's weekly target, spread sessions through the week, and scale down intensity when you are sick or very short of sleep rather than skipping everything.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If you cannot complete sessions because of pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness.
