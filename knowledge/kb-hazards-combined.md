## What happens
NASA stresses that the five hazards can make each other worse. For example, isolation and long work days disturb sleep; poor sleep raises stress and resting heart rate; distance means the crew must cope without fast help. This is why the app looks at several indicators together instead of one at a time.

## What astronauts can do
Treat sleep, exercise, hydration and mood as one system. Improving sleep often helps heart rate, mood and fatigue at the same time.

## How ASTRA HEALTH tracks it
Combined rules (such as the fatigue pattern: short sleep plus raised resting heart rate plus high fatigue or low mood) and the recovery-strain trend look for these linked changes.
