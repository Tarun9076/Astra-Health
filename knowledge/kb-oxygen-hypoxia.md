## What happens
Oxygen saturation (SpO₂) shows how much oxygen your blood carries; healthy values are usually 95 % or higher. NASA lists hypoxia, mildly low oxygen in body tissues, as a risk, for example linked to the lower-pressure atmospheres used to prevent decompression sickness during spacewalks. Poor sensor contact can also give falsely low readings.

## What astronauts can do
If a reading is low, warm your hands, sit still and measure again. Report shortness of breath, headache or confusion.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
Repeated readings of 90 % or lower, or any difficulty breathing, blue lips or confusion, are an emergency: follow the low-oxygen protocol.
