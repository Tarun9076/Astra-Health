## What happens
NASA lists behavioural changes and psychiatric disorders among spaceflight risks: isolation, distance, workload and other stressors can lower mood, raise anxiety and disturb sleep. NASA has also studied whether growing plants helps crews cope with isolation.

## What astronauts can do
Name the stress, talk to a crewmate, keep exercise and sleep regular, and use short breathing exercises (for example, breathe in for four counts and out for six). Small pleasant routines, such as a call home or tending plants, help.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If low mood or anxiety lasts more than a few days, you stop enjoying things, or you feel unable to cope. Thoughts of self-harm are an emergency: tell a crew member and follow the protocol immediately.
