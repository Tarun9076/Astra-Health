## What happens
The habitat is a sealed ecosystem. Temperature, pressure, lighting, noise, air quality (including carbon dioxide) and microbes all affect health and performance, and the immune system can respond differently in this setting. NASA studies how microbial communities change in spacecraft.

## What astronauts can do
Keep to cleaning and hygiene routines, report unusual smells, noise or temperature, and drink enough. Headache, poor concentration and tiredness can be early signs of stale air or heat.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If several crew members feel unwell at the same time, or symptoms start after an environmental warning. Use the Report button or tell the crew medical officer (CMO) if the problem is sudden, severe or getting worse. The app also flags it for the flight surgeon on the next link window.
