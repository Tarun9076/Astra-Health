## Your personal normal
Bodies differ: 80 bpm can be normal for one astronaut and high for another. The app learns your own normal from the median of your previous four weeks (skipping the last three days and days with alerts), and its spread, written σ (sigma). '+2.5σ' means 2.5 times your usual spread above your normal.

## Alerts and severity
Alerts come only from fixed, versioned rules: Attention means 'look at this soon'; Urgent means 'act now' and shows an emergency procedure. The AI assistant and the machine learning never change an alert's severity.

## Wellness index and AI insights
The wellness index (0–100) summarises your readings against your normal; it is not a clinical score. AI insights point out unusual days and slow drifts across several indicators, as an early heads-up.
