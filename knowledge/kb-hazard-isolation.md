## What happens
Small crews living in a confined space for months, far from family and friends, can experience changes in mood, sleep, motivation and how well the team works together. NASA notes that crews must be carefully chosen, trained and supported to work as a team for long periods.

## What astronauts can do
Keep a regular daily routine, protect your sleep, exercise, and make time for private calls or messages home when the link allows. Talk openly with crewmates and use the check-in honestly; a low mood score is useful information, not a failure.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If low mood, worry or irritability lasts more than a few days or affects your work or sleep, ask for a private conversation with the support team. For thoughts of self-harm, use the emergency protocol straight away.
