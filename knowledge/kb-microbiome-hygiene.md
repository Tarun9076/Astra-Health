## What happens
The microbes living on our skin and in our gut change in space, and NASA lists host-microorganism interactions as a risk because these changes may raise the chance of illness. Microbes also grow on habitat surfaces and hardware.

## What astronauts can do
Follow hygiene and cleaning schedules, keep food areas clean, and report skin problems, stomach upsets or unusual growth on surfaces.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
For a rash that spreads, a wound that looks infected, or vomiting or diarrhoea lasting more than a day.
