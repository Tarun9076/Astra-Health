## What happens
NASA lists inadequate sleep and irregular schedules as a risk: workload, noise, unusual light and shifting schedules disturb the body clock and lead to chronic sleep loss. Short sleep lowers alertness and mood and can raise resting heart rate. NASA's ARCHeR study uses wrist devices that record movement, sleep and light exposure.

## What astronauts can do
Keep fixed sleep and wake times, dim the lights and screens before bed, use the sleep station's light settings, and avoid caffeine late in the day. A short wind-down routine and breathing exercise can help you fall asleep.

## How ASTRA HEALTH tracks it
Sleep hours and quality from the check-in and wearable are compared with your own normal; several short nights in a row raise a sleep-deficit alert.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If you sleep less than about six hours for several nights, feel dangerously drowsy at work, or poor sleep comes with low mood.
