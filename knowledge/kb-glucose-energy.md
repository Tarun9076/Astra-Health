## What happens
Blood glucose rises after meals and falls with exercise and fasting. Low blood sugar can cause shakiness, sweating, hunger, irritability and confusion; persistently high values may point to stress, illness or changes in metabolism during long missions.

## What astronauts can do
Eat regular meals, have a snack before long exercise sessions, and recheck an unusual reading after a few minutes.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
A reading below 54 mg/dL, or low-sugar symptoms with confusion or drowsiness, is an emergency: follow the low-glucose protocol. Report repeated readings above 180 mg/dL.
