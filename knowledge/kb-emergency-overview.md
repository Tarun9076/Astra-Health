## What happens
Far from Earth, the crew must act first in an emergency. ASTRA HEALTH treats these as emergencies and shows the matching procedure at once, without waiting for any AI: chest pain or pressure; severe difficulty breathing; fainting; sudden confusion, weakness or trouble speaking; sudden loss of vision; severe bleeding; thoughts of self-harm.

## What astronauts can do
Stop what you are doing, call the crew medical officer and follow the numbered steps on screen. Do not wait to see whether it passes.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
The app creates an Urgent alert and queues a priority report for the flight surgeon automatically; the CMO leads care on board.
