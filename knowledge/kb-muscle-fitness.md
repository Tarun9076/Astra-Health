## What happens
With less gravity to work against, muscles and the heart lose strength and endurance. NASA lists reduced cardiorespiratory and musculoskeletal fitness as a risk, and studies exercise approaches such as training without a treadmill (the Zero T2 study).

## What astronauts can do
Complete the weekly exercise sessions in your mission profile. If you are behind, the app adds an exercise task. A falling strength score is a signal to talk with the exercise specialist, not to train harder alone.

## How ASTRA HEALTH tracks it
Weekly strength assessments and exercise counts are compared with your own history and your mission target; a steady decline raises an Attention alert.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
For sudden weakness, muscle pain that does not go away, or an injury during exercise.
