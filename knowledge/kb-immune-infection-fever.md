## What happens
Spaceflight stress changes how the immune system works. NASA reports that dormant viruses can reactivate and cause rashes and other symptoms, and studies immune markers in blood and saliva (Immune Biomarkers). A fever, a body temperature of about 38 °C or more, is a common sign that the body is fighting an infection.

## What astronauts can do
Rest, drink fluids, recheck your temperature in a few hours, and keep good hand hygiene to protect crewmates. Do not start any medication unless the CMO directs it.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
For a temperature of 39.5 °C or more, a fever lasting more than a day, or fever with a stiff neck, confusion, breathing difficulty or a spreading rash: follow the fever protocol.
