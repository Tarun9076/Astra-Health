## What happens
The Canadian Space Agency explains that the body's changes in space resemble accelerated ageing and the effects of an inactive lifestyle, so six months in orbit can show what takes years on Earth. Research on bones, the heart, blood vessels and the brain may help people with balance problems, osteoporosis, heart disease and type 2 diabetes.

## Standard measures
NASA collects a consistent set of health measurements from crews (Standard Measures) so future researchers can compare missions. Honest, regular check-ins add to this record.
