## What happens
After time in weightlessness the body has less circulating fluid and the blood vessels react more slowly, so standing up, or arriving in a new gravity field, can make you light-headed. The inner ear also has to re-learn which way is down, which can cause dizziness, unsteady walking and motion sickness.

## What astronauts can do
Stand up slowly, sit back down if you feel faint, drink enough fluid, and avoid sudden head movements in the first days after a gravity change.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If you actually faint, hit your head, or the dizziness comes with chest pain, a pounding heartbeat, confusion or trouble speaking. Fainting is an emergency: follow the fainting protocol.
