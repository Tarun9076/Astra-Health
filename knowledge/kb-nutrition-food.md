## What happens
NASA lists inadequate food and nutrition as a risk for deep-space missions: food must stay nutritious and appealing for years. NASA has studied how spaceflight changes food appeal and whether an enhanced diet helps the body adapt.

## What astronauts can do
Eat the planned meals even when appetite is low, keep variety, and report weight loss or loss of appetite in the check-in.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
For steady weight loss, persistent nausea, or symptoms of low blood sugar such as shakiness, sweating or confusion.
