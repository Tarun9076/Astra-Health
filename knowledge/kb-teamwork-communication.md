## What happens
NASA lists inadequate teamwork as a risk: small isolated crews can drift into misunderstandings and conflict, and long communication delays make support from Earth slower.

## What astronauts can do
Hold short daily crew check-ins, say early when something bothers you, and write clear messages to the ground that include what you need and by when.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
If conflict affects sleep, mood or safety, ask for a private session with the behavioural support team.
