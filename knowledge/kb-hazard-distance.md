## What happens
Mars is on average about 140 million miles from Earth. A lunar trip takes about three days; a Mars mission keeps a crew away for about three years. Messages take many minutes each way, resupply is impossible on short notice, and there is no quick evacuation, so the crew must handle medical problems on their own first.

## What astronauts can do
Rely on onboard procedures and the crew medical officer for anything urgent. Record symptoms and readings in the app so the flight surgeon gets a complete picture in one message.

## How ASTRA HEALTH tracks it
The app works fully offline on the habitat: rules, alerts and emergency procedures do not need Earth. Reports and alerts are queued and sent to the ground when the link is up.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
Anything Urgent goes to the CMO immediately; the ground is informed on the next link window. Non-urgent questions can wait for the daily report.
