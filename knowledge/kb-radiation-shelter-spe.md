## What happens
Solar storms can release bursts of energetic particles that raise radiation levels far above normal for hours to days. Shielding, such as the habitat's storm shelter surrounded by water and supplies, greatly reduces the dose. ASTRA HEALTH's demo replays a real solar event from May 2024 recorded by NASA.

## What astronauts can do
When the radiation alert appears, stop outside work, go to the shelter, keep your dosimeter on and stay until the all-clear. Record any symptoms afterwards.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
After every solar particle event, so your dose can be reviewed, and at once if you feel nauseous, vomit or feel unusually weak.
