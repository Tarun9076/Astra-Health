## What happens
Bones need loading to stay strong. In weightlessness and reduced gravity they lose mineral density, which NASA lists as a risk because it can make bones more brittle and raise the chance of fractures. NASA studies spine health with scans to improve countermeasures.

## What astronauts can do
Do the resistance exercise sessions; loading through the legs and hips matters most. Eat the planned diet, which is designed to support bone health.

## When to contact the flight surgeon
After any fall or impact, or for new pain in a bone, the back or a joint that does not settle.
