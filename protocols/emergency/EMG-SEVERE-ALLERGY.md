id: EMG-SEVERE-ALLERGY
title: Severe allergic reaction
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, trouble breathing or swallowing after a food, medicine or contact, hives all over the body with dizziness]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO) now** and say "severe allergy".
2. **Stop the cause** if you know it: stop eating, do not take more of a new medicine, move away from the substance.
3. The CMO **opens the onboard anaphylaxis checklist** and uses the emergency kit (including its epinephrine auto-injector) exactly as that checklist says.
4. **Lie down with your legs raised.** If breathing is hard, sit up instead.
5. **Measure** oxygen saturation, heart rate and blood pressure every 5 minutes.
6. **Do not use the Medication Guide** for this: allergy tablets are too slow for a severe reaction.
7. Symptoms can come back hours later: stay with a crewmate for the rest of the day.
8. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
