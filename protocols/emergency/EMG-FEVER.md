---
id: EMG-FEVER
title: High fever or very low body temperature
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [body temperature 39.5 °C or higher, body temperature 35.0 °C or lower]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Recheck the temperature** with the same thermometer to confirm the reading.
2. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO).**
3. **Rest** and stop strenuous tasks. For high temperature, remove extra layers; for low temperature, add layers and warm the person gradually.
4. **Limit close contact** with other crew and wash hands often, in case of infection in the closed habitat.
5. **Record symptoms** in the app (for example cough, sore throat, headache, rash, stomach upset, confusion).
6. **Measure** heart rate, blood pressure and oxygen saturation every 30 minutes, or as the CMO directs.
7. Do not take any medication unless the CMO directs it under the onboard checklist.
8. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
