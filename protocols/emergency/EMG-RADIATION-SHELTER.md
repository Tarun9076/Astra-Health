1. **Stop what you are doing and go to the designated storm shelter now.** If you are outside on EVA, return to the habitat by the fastest safe route.
2. **Take your personal dosimeter** and check that it is worn and recording.
3. **Account for every crew member** in the shelter and report the head count to mission control.
4. **Stay in the shelter** until mission control or the onboard alert gives the all-clear. Space-weather updates from Earth arrive after the light-time delay shown in the app.
5. **Log the time** you entered and left the shelter. The app records your dose for the event.
6. After the event, **complete the cognitive check** and report any symptoms (for example nausea, fatigue or headache) in the app.
