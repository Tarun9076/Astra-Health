id: EMG-NEURO-SYMPTOMS
title: Sudden confusion, weakness, trouble speaking or vision loss
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [sudden confusion, face drooping, arm or leg weakness, slurred speech, sudden vision loss, worst headache]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Note the time** the symptoms started, or when the person was last seen well. This matters for treatment decisions.
2. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO)** and keep the person still, secured and supervised. Do not give food, drink or medication.
3. **Check quickly:** is one side of the face drooping? Can they raise both arms? Is their speech slurred or strange? Can they see normally?
4. **Measure** blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, temperature and blood glucose. The app records them automatically; low blood sugar can look like a stroke.
5. The CMO **opens the onboard neurological checklist** and follows it.
6. A **priority report** with the times and readings is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
7. If the person **stops responding or breathing normally**, the CMO starts the onboard resuscitation procedure immediately.
