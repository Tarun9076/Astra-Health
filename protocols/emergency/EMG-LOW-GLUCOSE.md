---
id: EMG-LOW-GLUCOSE
title: Blood glucose very low
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [blood glucose below 54 mg/dL]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Stop what you are doing and sit down.**
2. **Tell a crew member and the crew medical officer (CMO).** Do not stay alone.
3. If you are **awake and able to swallow**, follow the onboard low-glucose checklist for fast-acting carbohydrate from the medical kit.
4. **Recheck glucose** when the checklist says, and record each reading in the app.
5. If the person becomes **drowsy, confused or unable to swallow**, the CMO starts the onboard emergency procedure at once. Give nothing by mouth.
6. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
