---
id: EMG-LOW-OXYGEN
title: Oxygen saturation critically low
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [oxygen saturation 90 percent or lower on repeat readings]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Stop activity** and sit or lie down.
2. **Check the reading is real:** warm hands, keep still, reseat the oximeter probe or move it to another finger, and measure again.
3. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO)** if the repeat reading is still 90% or lower, or at once if you feel short of breath, confused or have chest pain.
4. **Check the cabin:** report the module's CO₂ and oxygen readings to the crew. Other crew members feeling unwell may point to an environment problem rather than an individual one.
5. The CMO **opens the onboard respiratory checklist** and follows it, including any supplemental oxygen procedure.
6. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
