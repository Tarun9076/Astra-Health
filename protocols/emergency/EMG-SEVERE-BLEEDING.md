---
id: EMG-SEVERE-BLEEDING
title: Severe bleeding
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [severe bleeding, bleeding that won't stop, deep cut, coughing or vomiting blood]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Put on gloves if they are within reach**, then **press firmly and continuously** on the wound with a clean dressing. Do not lift it to look; add more dressing on top if blood soaks through.
2. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO)** and another crew member. In reduced gravity, contain loose blood droplets with absorbent material.
3. **Keep the person lying down** and secured, and keep them warm.
4. For **coughing or vomiting blood**, keep the person upright or on their side and do not give food or drink.
5. **Measure** heart rate, blood pressure and oxygen saturation; the app records them. A rising heart rate with falling blood pressure is a warning sign.
6. The CMO **opens the onboard trauma checklist** and follows it.
7. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
