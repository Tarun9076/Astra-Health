---
id: EMG-CHEST-PAIN
title: Chest pain or chest pressure
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [chest pain, chest pressure, chest tightness]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Stop all activity** and sit or lie down in a stable position. Do not exercise.
2. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO)** and another crew member to stay with you.
3. **Note the time** the pain started and describe it in the app (where it is, how strong 1–10, whether it spreads to the arm, jaw or back, and whether you feel short of breath, sweaty or sick).
4. **Measure** heart rate, blood pressure and oxygen saturation with the onboard devices. The app records them automatically.
5. The CMO **opens the onboard cardiac checklist** and follows it. Do not take any medication unless the CMO directs it under that checklist.
6. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
7. If the person **stops responding or breathing normally**, the CMO starts the onboard resuscitation procedure immediately.
