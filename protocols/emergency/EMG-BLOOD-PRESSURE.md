---
id: EMG-BLOOD-PRESSURE
title: Very high blood pressure
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [systolic 180 mmHg or higher on repeat readings, diastolic 120 mmHg or higher on repeat readings]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Stop activity and sit quietly** for 5 minutes, feet supported, arm at heart level.
2. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO).**
3. **Recheck blood pressure** with the cuff on the same arm, and note the time of each reading in the app.
4. **Report any symptoms** at once: severe headache, chest pain, shortness of breath, vision changes, confusion, weakness or numbness. Any of these makes this an emergency.
5. The CMO **opens the onboard cardiovascular checklist** and follows it. Do not take any medication unless the CMO directs it under that checklist.
6. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
