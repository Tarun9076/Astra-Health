**You are not alone, and asking for help is the right thing to do.**

1. **Tell a crew member or the crew medical officer (CMO) now.** If you can't say it out loud, press "Contact support" in the app and the CMO is notified.
2. **Stay with another crew member.** Nobody in crisis should be left alone.
3. The CMO **opens the onboard behavioural-health checklist** and follows it, including making the area safe.
4. A **private priority message** is sent to the mission's psychological support team on the next link window. Its content is visible only to you and the support team.
5. While you wait: slow your breathing (the guided breathing exercise in the Wellness Center can help), drink some water, and focus on one small, calm task.
