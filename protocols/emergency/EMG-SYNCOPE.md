---
id: EMG-SYNCOPE
title: Fainting or near-fainting
version: 1.0.0
severity: Urgent
appliesTo: [fainted, passed out, nearly fainted, sudden dizziness]
disclaimer: Illustrative prototype procedure. Replace with the mission's approved medical checklist.
---

1. **Lie the person down** and secure them against floating or falling (in reduced gravity, raise the legs if possible).
2. **Check responsiveness and breathing.** If they are not breathing normally, call the CMO and start the onboard resuscitation procedure.
3. **Call the crew medical officer (CMO).** Do not leave the person alone.
4. **Note the time**, what they were doing (for example standing up quickly, exercise, after a long task) and how long they were unresponsive.
5. **Measure** heart rate, blood pressure (lying and, only if the CMO agrees, sitting), oxygen saturation and blood glucose.
6. Keep them lying down until the CMO says otherwise. Give fluids only if they are fully awake and the CMO agrees.
7. A **priority report** is queued for the flight surgeon and sent on the next link window.
